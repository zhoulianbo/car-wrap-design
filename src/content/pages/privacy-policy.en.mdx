## Information We Handle

- **Account information:** email address, display name, profile image where provided, authentication records, account preferences, and sign-in session information. If you use a supported third-party sign-in option, we receive the profile information needed to create and authenticate your account.
- **Design content:** uploaded images, reference images, prompts, selected vehicle and design settings, editable project data, saved textures, previews, generated results, and publication or sharing settings.
- **Generation and credit records:** task identifiers, AI provider and model, task status, timestamps, errors, credit reservations, charges, and releases.
- **Transaction information:** order references, purchased products, amounts, currency, payment status, and billing or payer details returned by the payment provider. Payment credentials are processed by the checkout provider; our application does not store full card numbers or card security codes.
- **Technical information:** IP addresses, browser and device information, request and session records, and error or security logs processed when the service is accessed. Analytics or support tools may collect additional usage information when enabled, as described below.
- **Communications:** support requests, design reports, feedback, and information you provide while resolving a dispute or privacy request.

Do not upload identity documents, payment credentials, sensitive personal information, or confidential client material that is unnecessary for a wrap design. Obtain permission before uploading material about another person.

## Local Previews and Saved Files

An image selected only for the temporary preview on a vehicle page is processed in your browser. Selecting that preview alone does not save the image to your account or submit it for AI generation. Uploading an asset, saving an editor project, or submitting an AI reference image uses server storage and is a separate action.

Saved projects and uploaded assets use our application database and Cloudflare R2 object storage. A project omitted from the gallery is not necessarily confidential: image files can be delivered through public storage URLs, and anyone who obtains a working URL may access the file. Do not treat unlisted projects or difficult-to-guess URLs as secure storage for sensitive information.

## AI Processing and Service Providers

When you submit an AI task, the selected API provider receives the prompt, design instructions and settings, vehicle template, and any reference images or URLs needed to generate the result. We receive the output and task-status information and save records associated with your account. The current model and API source are described in the <Link href="/terms-of-service#ai-models" className="text-primary font-medium underline underline-offset-4">AI Models and API Providers section of our Terms</Link>.

OpenAI's published API policy states that API data is not used to train its models by default, unless the API customer opts in to data sharing. It may retain content and related records for abuse monitoring, service operation, or legal obligations under its [API data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data). This policy does not promise zero retention. Avoid submitting material you are not authorized to send to an external provider.

Other recipients may include Cloudflare for hosting, delivery, and file storage; the payment provider identified at checkout for transactions and fraud checks; enabled sign-in and email providers for account services; and enabled analytics or customer-support providers. We share information needed for the relevant service. We may also disclose relevant records to comply with lawful requests, investigate fraud or abuse, protect rights and safety, or support a business transfer subject to applicable privacy obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

## Public Gallery and Share Links

For accounts that have not purchased a credit pack, AI generations are open to gallery review. Eligible paid accounts can turn off “Open to gallery” before generating. Approved public designs may appear in the gallery and site showcases, be indexed by search engines, and be previewed, downloaded, or remixed by other users. Public design details may include the title, vehicle, artwork, and other publication fields. Prompts are displayed only where the publication is configured to make them public.

Enabling a share link makes its preview available to anyone with the link, without requiring access to your account. A shared preview does not expose the editable project document through the sharing page. You can disable or regenerate a share link using the available controls. Removing a design or disabling a link does not delete copies already downloaded by others, and does not necessarily immediately remove cached images or existing storage URLs.

## Retention and Deletion

Account and saved-content records remain available while needed to operate your account and the features you use. Generation, credit, payment, support, and safety records may be retained to resolve failures or disputes, prevent abuse, and meet accounting or legal requirements. Retention depends on the record and purpose; we do not promise that all data is automatically erased after a fixed number of days.

Deleting a project removes it from your active project list and withdraws linked gallery publications and its share page. Some project and generation records may remain in the database, and stored files may remain where other records still reference them. Uploaded source assets have separate deletion controls; removing a project does not necessarily remove those assets. Deletion can affect other designs that use the same asset.

For account closure or a broader deletion request, email support. We may retain information required by law or necessary for fraud prevention, payment reconciliation, or legal claims, and will explain relevant limitations when responding. Provider-held records, cached copies, and copies already saved by other users are not all under our direct control.
